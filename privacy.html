Privacy policy

How Sivels Finance may collect, use, share and protect personal information.

Company review required: This draft is informed by the privacy and disclosure policy published by Orange Retail Finance India Private Limited, but is written for Sivels Finance. Confirm the Sivels legal entity, data practices, retention periods, grievance contact and regulatory requirements with the company’s legal and compliance teams before publication.

Sivels Finance recognizes the importance of privacy, confidentiality and security of personal information. This policy describes how personal information may be collected, used, disclosed, stored, secured and disposed of when you visit our website, contact us or apply for a finance product.

Information we may collect

Depending on your interactions with Sivels Finance and the product requested, information may include:

  • Demographic, identity and contact details such as name, date of birth, address, phone number, email address, occupation, age and language.
  • Authentication and identity documents such as signatures, PAN, Aadhaar or other accepted identity and address proofs.
  • Financial and account details such as bank account information, statements, income and proof of income, loan records, and credit information obtained where permitted.
  • Educational, employment, professional and business information relevant to assessing and servicing an application.
  • Property information and documents relevant to a home loan or loan against property.
  • Device and usage information such as device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, network information, logs and interactions with our digital services.
  • Communications with us, including enquiry details, service requests, complaints and testimonials you choose to provide.

Sources and collection

We may collect personal information directly from you during discussions with our representatives, through application or enquiry forms, through this website or other digital services, and from third parties where permitted and relevant to the service. These sources may include credit information companies, identity verification providers, banks or other financial institutions, subject to applicable requirements.

How information may be used

We may use information to respond to enquiries, assess creditworthiness and repayment capacity, complete KYC and identity checks, process and service loan applications, communicate with customers, authenticate payments, prevent fraud, meet legal and regulatory obligations, resolve complaints, and maintain business records. Information will be used for the purposes for which it was collected or another lawful and compatible purpose.

Sharing of information

We do not intend to disclose personal or sensitive personal information except with an appropriate legal basis. Information may be shared with service providers supporting loan processing, identity verification, credit assessment, customer communications, payment processing, technology and operations; with credit information companies or financial institutions where permitted or required; with regulators, courts, law enforcement or other authorities when legally required; or to protect rights, safety and property. Where consent is required, we will seek it for the relevant sharing.

Storage, transfer and security

We take reasonable physical, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse. Access is limited to personnel and service providers who need the information for their work and are expected to safeguard it. Information is retained for the period required for the purposes described in this policy, applicable law, regulatory directions and legitimate business needs.

Information may be processed by service providers. Any cross-border transfer, if applicable, will be handled in accordance with applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

Cookies and digital services

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences, understand website usage and improve services. You may block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled. Third-party services may use technologies governed by their own privacy notices.

Your requests and concerns

You may contact Sivels Finance to raise a privacy concern or request correction of information, subject to identity verification, applicable law and record-retention requirements. We may need to retain information to meet legal, regulatory, security or contractual duties.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page. Sivels should add an effective date when this policy is approved.
