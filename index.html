Questions, answered

Good to know before you apply.

Still unsure? Our team is happy to walk you through it.

Does sending an enquiry mean my loan is approved?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Any application is subject to eligibility, verification, credit appraisal and formal approval by Sivels Finance.

What documents will I need?

Usually identity and address proof, income or business proof, bank statements and—for secured loans—property documents. Your team will confirm the exact list for your situation.

Can I get a loan without property as security?

Unsecured micro loans and some business loans do not require property collateral. Eligibility is assessed on income, business activity and repayment capacity.

How is my interest rate decided?

Rates depend on the product, your profile and company policy. The annualised rate, charges and repayment schedule will be stated in your written offer and loan agreement.
