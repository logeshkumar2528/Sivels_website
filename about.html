02 / THE THANThI GROUP LEGACY

A NAME THAT REACHES GENERATIONS

Rooted in service.
Growing with the times.

In 1942, the late S. P. Adithanar, a barrister who believed news should reach the common Tamil reader in simple language, launched Dina Thanthi (Daily Thanthi) from Madurai. Inspired by popular mass-circulation newspapers abroad, he built a daily that spoke directly to ordinary people, and it grew into one of the most widely read Tamil dailies.

After his passing in 1981, the group was led by his son, the late Padma Shri Dr. B. Sivanthi Adithanar, who steered it through decades of growth until 2013. Under this leadership the group expanded well beyond one newspaper.

Today the Thanthi Group is a diversified media and business family with interests that include Daily Thanthi, the evening daily Maalai Malar, DT Next, Thanthi TV, Hello FM, and the Tamil publications Rani, Rani Muthu and Gokulam Kathir, along with education, realty and other ventures. Sivels Finance carries that legacy of public trust into financial services.
